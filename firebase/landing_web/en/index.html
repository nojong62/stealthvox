FAQ

What is StealthVox?

An AI speaking app that turns the conversations you actually have into language study material. You can speak in your own language; the AI rewrites what you said as natural sentences in the language you are learning, then you practice them with tutoring and shadowing.

Can I practice speaking Korean or English without a conversation partner?

Yes. In Circle Talk you chat with a group of AI friends about topics you like, and in Scenario Talk you rehearse real situations such as airport check-in or ordering at a restaurant.

Can I learn a language by calling a friend?

Yes. Invite a friend with Duo and call. Even if you chat in your native language, what you both said becomes study material in the language you are learning. With a friend who speaks a different language, use the AI-interpreted call so each of you speaks your own language.

How is it different from other AI conversation apps?

Instead of a fixed curriculum, it starts from what you actually said. And not only AI chats — real calls with friends become study material too.

Which languages are supported?

12 languages: Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Italian and Dutch.

Where can I download it?

StealthVox is available for Android on Google Play.
