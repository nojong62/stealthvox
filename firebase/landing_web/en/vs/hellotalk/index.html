FAQ

What is the main difference between HelloTalk and StealthVox?

HelloTalk is a language-exchange community: you find native speakers and trade text and voice messages. StealthVox is an AI speaking app: you talk with AI friends, in practice scenarios, or on a call with a friend you already know, and the conversation becomes study material you can practice with tutoring and shadowing.

Is there an alternative to HelloTalk if I don't want to talk to strangers?

StealthVox lets you practice with AI friends any time, or call a friend you already know with Duo. If you speak different languages, the AI-interpreted call lets each of you speak your own language.

Which app is better for learning Korean as a K-pop fan?

If you want to meet many new Korean speakers, HelloTalk's community is large. If you want to turn your own sentences into natural Korean and practice saying them, or call a Korean friend with AI interpretation, StealthVox fits.
