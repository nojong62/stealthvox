## 한 줄 소개 (60자 안쪽)

## 짧은 설명 (150자 안쪽)
Talk freely in your own language. StealthVox rewrites it as natural sentences in the language you're learning, then helps you say them with tutoring and shadowing.

## 긴 설명
StealthVox is an Android speaking-practice app for people who know some words but freeze when they have to talk.

Instead of fixed dialogues, it starts from what you actually wanted to say:
1. Speak in your own language (or chat with AI friends in Circle Talk, or rehearse real situations in Scenario Talk).
2. The AI rewrites what you said as natural sentences in the language you're learning, and shows other ways a native might say it.
3. Practice out loud with instant tutoring on your own sentences and shadowing with a native-style voice.

You can also call a friend with Duo: a direct call whose conversation becomes study material, or an AI-interpreted call where each person speaks their own language.

Languages: 12 (Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch).
Pricing: no subscription. Pay by time: 10 minutes for 400 KRW, or 1 hour for 2,000 KRW (Google Play prices vary by country). A one-time 10-minute study room trial needs no sign-up.
Android only for now.
