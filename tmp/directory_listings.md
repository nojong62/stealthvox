## 설명 (짧은 것, 260자)
StealthVox is an AI speaking-practice app for 12 languages. Talk with AI friends, practice real-life scenarios, or call a friend. Speak in the language you're comfortable with, and the AI rewrites it as natural sentences in the language you're learning, then coaches you through saying them out loud.

## 설명 (긴 것)
Most speaking apps give you sentences to memorize. StealthVox does the opposite: what you actually said becomes the lesson.

- Circle Talk: chat with several AI friends about topics you like.
- Scenario Talk: rehearse airports, restaurants, interviews with an AI partner.
- Duo: invite a friend to a call. Between two languages, AI interprets live so each person speaks their own language. After the call, what you both said turns into study material.
- Native-phrasing comparison: see your sentence next to how a native would say it.
- Tutoring, essay, shadowing, and Snap Echoing practice built on your own conversations.

Android. 12 languages. Free to install, with a 10-minute Study Room trial.

## 주요 기능 (불릿 5개)
1. Your own conversations become your textbook
2. Speak in your native language, get natural target-language sentences
3. AI friends and real-life scenario role-play
4. Live AI interpreter calls between two people
5. Shadowing and echo practice on your own sentences
